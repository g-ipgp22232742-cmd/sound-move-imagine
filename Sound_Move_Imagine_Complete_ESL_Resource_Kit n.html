Guide for educators

Teacher's Corner

A practical guide to using songs and poems to make primary ESL learning active and meaningful.

Resource Overview

Target learners

Year 3 primary ESL learners. Adapt language and support to the actual class.

Duration

30 minutes per activity.

Skills and materials

Listening, speaking, reading, writing, vocabulary, rhythm and creative expression. Suggested materials: device/speakers, safe classroom objects, paper and pencils.

Teaching Principles

🎧 Model first

Demonstrate pronunciation, rhythm and instructions.

🪜 Scaffold learning

Use gestures, visuals, word banks and sentence frames.

🤝 Include everyone

Offer seated actions, non-performing roles and alternative ways to respond.

Assessment Guidance

  • Observe whether pupils respond to instructions and use target words.
  • Listen for understandable vocabulary and simple sentences, not accent perfection.
  • Use the quizzes to check comprehension and review incorrect answers together.
  • For performances, assess participation, connection to meaning, expression and teamwork.
Safety and accessibility: Keep movement within personal space, use objects safely, avoid excessive volume and allow pupils to point, speak, draw or work with a partner.

Suggested References

Asher, J. J. (1969). The total physical response approach to second language learning. The Modern Language Journal, 53(1), 3–17.

Cameron, L. (2001). Teaching languages to young learners. Cambridge University Press.

Murphey, T. (1992). Music and song. Oxford University Press.

Shin, J. K., & Crandall, J. (2014). Teaching young learners English: From theory to practice. National Geographic Learning.

Check your institution's referencing requirements and verify publication details before academic submission.

Activity 1 • 30 minutes

🔎 The Sound Detective Mission

Listen carefully. Move your body. Solve the sound mystery!

Objectives: identify four sound-action words, follow spoken instructions and describe sounds using simple English sentences.

1. Meet the Sound Words

👏
CLAP

Clap your hands two times.

👣
STOMP

Stomp your feet gently on the floor.

👆
TAP

Tap your desk softly with your fingers.

🪇
SHAKE

Shake a safe sound-making object.

🤫
WHISPER

Say a word using a very quiet voice.

🔊
LOUD / SOFT

Make a sound loudly, then softly.

2. The Sound Detective Song

Original lyrics. Sing to a simple teacher-created melody or read rhythmically.

Verse 1 Detective, detective, listen to the sound, What can you hear all around? Clap your hands, one, two, three, Is that the clue you hear from me? Verse 2 Stomp your feet, tap the floor, Shake your hands and listen for more. Loud or soft, fast or slow, Follow the clues and off we go! Chorus Listen, listen, what do you hear? A little sound is hiding near! Clap, stomp, tap and play, Find the sound and save the day!

3. Mystery Sound Challenge

Choose the best answer for each clue.

1. I make a sound with my hands. I can do it twice. What am I?

2. I use my feet to make a strong sound on the floor. What can I do?

3. I move a sound-maker to create a sound. What action am I doing?

4. Which word means to speak very quietly?

5. “My sound is not loud. It is ____.”

4. Speaking Mission

Complete a sentence and say it to a partner:

  • I can ______ my hands.
  • The sound is ______.
  • I can ______ the shaker.

Challenge: Create one mystery clue for your classmates.

Activity 2 • 30 minutes

🎭 The Emotion Soundtrack

Feel the words. Express the feelings. Bring poetry to life!

Objectives: identify emotion words, express feelings through voice and facial expression, and recite lines with an appropriate tone.

1. A Little Cloud's Feelings

☁️ A Little Cloud's Feelings I am a little cloud up high, Floating softly through the sky. Sometimes happy, light and bright, Dancing in the warm sunlight. Sometimes worried, grey and small, Wondering if the rain will fall. Then excited, I drift away, Chasing rainbows as I play. Now I feel calm, soft and slow, Watching flowers start to grow. I feel proud of what I do, Sharing raindrops, fresh and new!

Think and talk: How does the cloud feel? Which words help you know? What happens in the poem?

2. Emotion Word Cards

😊
HAPPY

Smile and speak cheerfully.

😟
WORRIED

Use a concerned expression.

🤩
EXCITED

Use an energetic voice.

😌
CALM

Speak slowly and gently.

😢
SAD

Use a quiet, gentle voice.

🥇
PROUD

Stand tall and speak clearly.

3. Emotion Soundtrack Challenge

  1. Work with a small group and choose one emotion.
  2. Select two lines from the poem.
  3. Practise reading the lines with a suitable voice and facial expression.
  4. Add a safe sound effect using your voice, body percussion or an instrument.
  5. Perform for the class. The audience guesses the emotion.
Speaking frames:
  • The cloud feels ______.
  • I think the cloud feels ______ because ______.
  • I will use a ______ voice to express this feeling.

4. Emotion Check Quiz

1. Which word means feeling pleased or cheerful?

2. Which emotion means peaceful and relaxed?

3. Which voice best suits “excited”?

4. “I feel ______ because I did my best.”

5. What helps an audience understand a poem's feeling?

Activity 3 • 30 minutes

🌱 The Poetry Rescue Lab

Rescue the missing words. Help the garden poem bloom!

Objectives: use context clues, select suitable words and create a two-line ending with support from a word bank.

1. The Garden That Lost Its Words

Some words have disappeared. Use the word bank to help the poem bloom again.

🌼 The Garden That Lost Its Words The garden wakes up, fresh and _____ (1), The flowers stretch towards the _____ (2). Tiny _____ (3) sleep under the ground, While bees and butterflies fly all around. The gentle winds begin to _____ (4), The leaves make music, a soft little _____ (5). The garden is beautiful, for you and _____ (6), A magical place where all creatures can _____ (7). A butterfly dances with a bright little _____ (8), And the garden smiles in the morning light.

2. Word Bank

🌿 GREEN

Colour of healthy leaves

☀️ LIGHT

Brightness from the sun

🌱 SEEDS

Small parts that grow into plants

💨 BLOW

To move air

🤫 WHISPER

A very quiet voice or sound

🧒 ME

The person speaking about themself

🎶 SING

Make music with your voice

🦋 WING

A part an insect or bird uses to fly

3. Rescue the Poem Quiz

1. The garden wakes up, fresh and ____.

2. The flowers stretch towards the ____.

3. Tiny ____ sleep under the ground.

4. The gentle winds begin to ____.

5. The leaves make music, a soft little ____.

6. A butterfly dances with a bright little ____.

Discuss all poem blanks with the teacher. Context clues and the meaning of the full line matter.
